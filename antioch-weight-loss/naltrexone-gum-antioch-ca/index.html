    How Naltrexone Gum Works for Patients in Antioch, CA

    How Naltrexone Gum Works in Antioch

    Naltrexone blocks opioid receptors, and the 50 mg tablet has been FDA-approved for alcohol and opioid use disorder for decades. In gum form, a compounding pharmacy blends a measured dose into a chewable base. While you chew, part of the dose may pass through the cheek lining and the remainder is swallowed, though nobody has measured that split well. That is the idea. Nobody has shown the gum works better than a tablet.

    Who Naltrexone Gum Is For

    Adults who would be reasonable candidates for naltrexone but dislike swallowing pills are the main group. A few patients want it in a low dose wellness role. Others are working on cravings and want something they can chew right before a known trigger, like a Friday evening or a long drive home. Opioid users are not candidates. Significant liver disease usually points to a different plan.

    Dosing and Use in Antioch

    Your provider sets the strength of each piece and how often to use it. A typical instruction is to chew slowly for roughly 20 to 30 minutes and then discard the piece rather than swallowing it. Some plans use one piece daily at the same time. Targeted plans use a piece shortly before a known trigger, with a written cap on how many pieces a day. Compounded strengths vary, so follow the label exactly.

    What to Expect in the First Weeks

    The first week is mostly about learning the routine. Mild nausea and headache are common early on with naltrexone in any format, and a handful of people sleep lightly for a few nights. Most of it settles by week two. The taste takes some getting used to. At a check-in around week two, your provider asks how many pieces you are using, whether the timing works and whether your gums feel sore.

    Side Effects of Naltrexone Gum in Antioch

    The side effects match other forms of naltrexone and include nausea, headache, dizziness and trouble sleeping. Chewing brings a couple of extras, such as a bitter aftertaste and sore gums. Opioids are the serious concern. Naltrexone can stop opioid pain medicine from working and can trigger withdrawal in anyone who uses opioids, which is why the screening is so specific. Report yellowing skin, dark urine or severe stomach pain right away.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your questionnaire asks about current medications, opioid use, liver health, pregnancy and breastfeeding, plus any major medical conditions. Tramadol and codeine cough syrup are asked about by name. A recent liver panel showing ALT and AST may be requested when your history raises a flag. Jaw trouble matters too. Anyone with TMJ pain or fresh dental work may do better on capsules.

    How Naltrexone Gum Compares in Antioch

    Standard naltrexone tablets have decades of research and FDA approval for alcohol and opioid use disorder. LDN capsules sit at the other end, compounded and off-label at a few milligrams a night. Gum is mainly a preference format, newer and much less studied, picked by people who find pills hard to take. Weight loss is a different story. GLP-1 medications like semaglutide and tirzepatide have much better weight loss data than naltrexone in any form.
