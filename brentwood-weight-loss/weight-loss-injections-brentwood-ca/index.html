    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Brentwood, CA

    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Brentwood

    Most modern weight loss shots are GLP-1 medicines. They copy a hormone your gut releases after eating, which slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps regulate blood sugar after a meal. Semaglutide acts on GLP-1 alone. Tirzepatide adds the GIP receptor, and studies have shown greater average weight loss with it.

    Who Weight Loss Injections Are For

    FDA-approved injections are for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. They work alongside diet and exercise. They are not for pregnancy or certain thyroid cancer histories.

    Injection Dosing Schedule in Brentwood

    Every weekly injection starts low. Wegovy starts at 0.25 mg and moves up every four weeks to 0.5, 1, 1.7 and finally 2.4 mg, with some patients holding at 1.7 mg. Zepbound begins at 2.5 mg and can go up by 2.5 mg at a time to a top of 15 mg. Same day each week.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Appetite often drops in the first week. Weight change during the first month is usually modest because the starting dose is low, and nausea is most common in the day or two after each injection. Large trials reported average losses near 15 percent with semaglutide 2.4 mg over 68 weeks and near 20 percent with tirzepatide 15 mg over 72 weeks. Results vary.

    Injection Side Effects in Brentwood

    Nausea is the classic one. Others include constipation, diarrhea, heartburn and a small red bump where the needle went in, mostly early and around dose increases. Smaller meals, protein first and plenty of water help most people. Rarer serious risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems and dehydration-related kidney strain.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with BMI and medical history. Your provider then reviews every medication you take, looking hardest at insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills, since tirzepatide can make oral contraceptives less reliable after dose changes. Medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 in your family rules out GLP-1 injections. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are too.

    Brand-Name vs Compounded Injections in Brentwood

    Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for weight management. Ozempic and Mounjaro contain the same active ingredients but are FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes, not weight loss alone. Compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide are prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies and are not FDA-approved, so they have not gone through the same review for safety, effectiveness and manufacturing quality. Some patients choose them for availability.
