    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Brentwood, CA

    How NAD+ Works in Brentwood

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. Cells use it to move electrons while making ATP, and enzymes like sirtuins and PARPs burn through it while repairing DNA and handling stress. Levels decline with age. A subcutaneous injection places NAD+ under the skin, where it is absorbed into the bloodstream without passing through the digestive tract, though how much reaches each tissue is still being studied.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, mental clarity, exercise recovery and healthy aging are the usual candidates. People on semaglutide or tirzepatide sometimes add it. NAD+ does not treat disease or cause weight loss, and pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer or serious liver or kidney disease usually rule it out.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Brentwood

    Small shots, slow push. Many compounded protocols begin around 50 milligrams two or three times a week and rise gradually, often toward 100 milligrams or more, as tolerance builds. Belly and thigh are the usual sites. Pushing the plunger over about a minute cuts down on flushing.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. Many people feel warmth, flushing, mild nausea or a tight feeling right after injecting, and it usually fades within minutes. By the end of the first month, responders describe more even energy, and plenty of people feel no difference. Doses change based on your notes.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Brentwood

    Flushing leads the list. Other frequent ones are stinging at the site, nausea, headache and feeling lightheaded. Too much too quickly can cause chest pressure, cramping or palpitations, which typically ease with a smaller dose and a slower push. Hives or trouble breathing mean call 911.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. Expect questions on cancer, pregnancy, organ health, heart rhythm and whether you use NMN, NR or high-dose niacin. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Brentwood

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. An NAD+ drip delivers a larger amount in one sitting but can take two to four hours, and many people feel more nausea and cramping during the infusion. The nasal spray is needle-free. NMN and NR capsules work differently, as building blocks.
