    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Brentwood, CA

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Brentwood

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. Lower DHT protects follicles from the slow miniaturization behind pattern loss. Minoxidil adds something different. It extends the growth phase of each hair and supports thicker strands, likely by improving blood flow and signaling at the follicle.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. It works best when started early, while follicles are still producing hair, because areas that have been smooth for years rarely come back with any medicine. Postmenopausal women sometimes use finasteride off-label. It is never used in women who might become pregnant.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Brentwood

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil, often 1.25 to 2.5 mg, is an off-label daily pill some men prefer to avoid the mess. One spray daily also works. A compounded topical that combines finasteride and minoxidil goes on the scalp once a day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. The early shed is common and usually temporary. By months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening at the crown. Long-term trials showed most men held their hair for years. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Brentwood

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. These usually resolve after stopping, and rare mood changes, including low mood, should be reported to your provider right away. Topical minoxidil can itch or flake. Oral minoxidil can cause body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat. Topical finasteride may lower whole-body exposure.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. They also review every medication and supplement, and they ask whether you plan to have children soon, since finasteride can slightly lower sperm count in some men. PSA results drop by about half. Women who could become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Brentwood

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. Dutasteride + minoxidil is a stronger, off-label option, usually considered after a year on finasteride without enough change in the photos. For men avoiding DHT blockers, there is a minoxidil and GHK-Cu blend. Minoxidil alone helps, but it does nothing about DHT.
