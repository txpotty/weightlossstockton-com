    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Stockton, CA

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Stockton

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. With less DHT around, the follicles on the crown and temples stop shrinking as quickly, and many hold their size for years instead of fading out. Minoxidil adds something different. It extends the growth phase of each hair and supports thicker strands, likely by improving blood flow and signaling at the follicle.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. It works best when started early, while follicles are still producing hair, because areas that have been smooth for years rarely come back with any medicine. Some women past menopause use it off-label. It is never used in women who might become pregnant.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Stockton

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Minoxidil comes as a twice-daily topical or a low-dose off-label tablet. One spray daily also works. The combination spray is applied to the scalp each day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. The early shed is common and usually temporary. Around month six, density starts to improve. In the original finasteride trials, most men kept their hair over five years, and many saw regrowth. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Stockton

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. These usually resolve after stopping, and rare mood changes, including low mood, should be reported to your provider right away. Topical minoxidil can itch or flake. Oral minoxidil can cause body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat. The spray may reduce systemic side effects.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. Expect questions about your medication list and family plans, because both can shape the choice. Finasteride lowers PSA readings. Women who could become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Stockton

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. When finasteride falls short, dutasteride is the usual next step, off-label for hair. For men avoiding DHT blockers, there is a minoxidil and GHK-Cu blend. Minoxidil by itself leaves the hormonal cause untreated.
