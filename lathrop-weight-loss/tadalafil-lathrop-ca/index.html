    How Tadalafil Works in Lathrop

    Tadalafil is a PDE5 inhibitor, the same drug class as sildenafil. Blocking PDE5 keeps more cGMP around, and cGMP relaxes the smooth muscle in penile blood vessels so blood flows in more easily when you are aroused. It clears slowly, with a half-life near 17.5 hours. That is why one dose can last a day and a half. Arousal is still required.

    Who Tadalafil Is For

    Men with ED who want flexibility tend to like tadalafil. As-needed tadalafil works well for men who want more than the four to five hours sildenafil offers. Daily dosing suits men who have sex twice a week or more. A daily 5 mg tablet can handle ED and the urinary symptoms of BPH at the same time, because tadalafil carries FDA approval for both. Your provider makes the final call.

    Tadalafil Dosing Options in Lathrop

    As-needed dosing usually starts at 10 mg, taken 30 minutes to two hours before sex. The range runs from 5 mg to 20 mg. Daily tadalafil is 2.5 or 5 mg at about the same time each day, food or no food, reaching a steady level in roughly five days. One dose per day, either way. Unlike sildenafil, a heavy meal barely changes it.

    What to Expect in the First Weeks

    With as-needed tadalafil, most men notice a difference on the first or second try. Daily users usually feel it settle in by the end of week one. The first week or two can bring headaches, heartburn or a dull lower back ache, and those often fade as you keep taking it. Keep a few notes. After about a month, your provider reviews how it is going.

    Tadalafil Side Effects in Lathrop

    Headache, indigestion, back pain and muscle aches are the most common side effects. The back pain tends to show up 12 to 24 hours after a dose and usually fades within two days. Some men also get a flushed face or a stuffy nose. An erection lasting more than four hours, sudden vision loss or sudden hearing loss is an emergency and needs care right away, not a message. Rare, but real.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates in any form rule out tadalafil, including nitroglycerin and poppers, and so does riociguat. A recent heart attack or stroke, chest pain at rest and uncontrolled blood pressure all call for a closer look. Severe liver or kidney disease changes the dose. With ketoconazole, ritonavir or similar drugs, as-needed tadalafil is capped at 10 mg every 72 hours. Your provider also asks whether you can handle moderate exertion without chest pain.

    Tadalafil Compared to Sildenafil and Other Options in Lathrop

    Sildenafil works for four to five hours and is slowed by a heavy meal. Tadalafil covers up to 36 hours, meal or no meal. Compounded chewables, troches and rapid dissolve tablets often contain tadalafil too, but compounded versions are not FDA-approved, while generic tadalafil tablets are. Back pain is more common with tadalafil. Your provider weighs those trade-offs with you.
