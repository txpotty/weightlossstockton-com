    How Zepbound Treatment Works in Tracy, CA

    How Zepbound Works in Tracy

    Tirzepatide acts on two gut hormone receptors. GIP and GLP-1 together help regulate appetite, slow how fast the stomach empties and improve the way the body handles blood sugar after a meal. Many notice fewer cravings and smaller portions. You inject it once weekly into the belly, thigh or upper arm.

    Who Zepbound Is For

    Zepbound is approved for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. It is also approved for adults with a BMI of 27 or higher who have a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea. A separate approval covers moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. It is not approved for children.

    Zepbound Dosing Schedule in Tracy

    Everyone starts at 2.5 mg. The 2.5 mg dose is a starter dose meant to help your body adjust, and it is not where most of the effect comes from. After four weeks the dose usually moves up in 2.5 mg steps to 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5 and up to 15 mg, with at least four weeks at each. Maintenance doses are typically 5, 10 or 15 mg.

    What to Expect Week by Week in Tracy

    The first month is mostly quiet. Appetite often drops within days, while nausea or an upset stomach shows up most in the day or two after each injection and after every dose increase. In the SURMOUNT-1 trial, adults on the highest dose lost about 20 percent of body weight on average over 72 weeks. Individual results vary.

    Zepbound Side Effects

    Nausea and diarrhea come up most. Other common effects include constipation, heartburn, belly pain and mild injection site reactions, and they usually fade as your body adjusts. Serious but less common risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, kidney injury from dehydration and severe allergic reactions. Zepbound carries a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening in Tracy

    Your provider starts with height, weight and BMI. They then go through your history and medications, looking hardest at insulin, glipizide-type drugs and oral contraceptives. Anyone with medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 in their family is not a candidate, and Zepbound is not used while pregnant. Labs may be requested.

    Zepbound Compared With Related Options in Tracy

    Zepbound and Mounjaro are the same medicine. Zepbound carries the weight management label and Mounjaro carries the type 2 diabetes label, which matters for insurance and for how your provider writes the prescription. In the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, tirzepatide produced more average weight loss than Wegovy. Compounded tirzepatide is not FDA-approved and is a different product from Zepbound.
