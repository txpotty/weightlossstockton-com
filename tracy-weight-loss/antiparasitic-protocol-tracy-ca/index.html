    How the Antiparasitic Protocol Works in Tracy

    The protocol starts with your symptoms and possible exposures, not with a pill. Your provider considers which parasites are realistic given your travel, pets, food and water, plus anyone at home with the same symptoms. Then comes a testing decision. You may be asked to get a stool ova and parasite test or a specific antigen test before treatment. Treatment uses prescription anti-parasitics with established uses, and a follow-up confirms symptoms are gone.

    Who This Protocol Is For

    Adults with symptoms or exposures that raise a fair concern for a parasite are the right fit. The most common case is nighttime anal itching in a home with school-age children, which usually points to pinworm. Others have traveled somewhere intestinal worms are common or already have a positive test from another provider. It is not a detox. It has no role in weight loss either.

    Dosing and Schedule in Tracy

    The schedule depends on the drug and the parasite. Pinworm is commonly treated with one dose of mebendazole 100 mg or albendazole 400 mg, repeated after two weeks to catch worms that hatched after the first dose. Ivermectin is dosed by weight. It is usually given as one dose or a short course of one to two days. Your provider writes out exact dates for every dose.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Most courses take one to three days, sometimes with a repeat dose two weeks later. Some people feel a little queasy or tired for a day after the pills. With pinworm, the itching usually eases within a few days, and the remaining irritation can take another week to settle. Week two is the repeat dose. Around week four, your provider may ask for a symptom update or a repeat test.

    Side Effects and Precautions in Tracy

    Nausea, abdominal pain, diarrhea and headache are the side effects people mention most often. With ivermectin, a temporary rash or itch sometimes appears as the parasites die. Mebendazole and albendazole are poorly absorbed into the blood when used for intestinal worms, which keeps most side effects mild at short courses. Pregnancy changes things. Several of these drugs are not used during pregnancy, and the provider will ask.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your intake asks when symptoms started, where you have traveled in the past year and whether pets or family members are involved. Upload any previous stool test results. Providers also screen for pregnancy, breastfeeding, liver disease and current medications that could interact, such as warfarin with ivermectin. Severe abdominal pain, blood in the stool or a high fever mean in-person care. Some people are asked to finish testing before any medication is prescribed.

    How the Protocol Compares to Over-the-Counter Cleanses in Tracy

    Herbal parasite cleanses sold online are not regulated as medications. Doses differ between brands and even between batches, and few have been studied against any particular parasite. Prescription drugs come with set dosing. Stockton Weight Loss also offers ivermectin and mebendazole on their own, and each one is matched to a different set of infections. Your provider picks one drug rather than layering several products.
