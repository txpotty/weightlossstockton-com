    How ED Medication Works in Tracy

    An erection depends on blood flowing into the penis faster than it drains out, and the enzyme PDE5 is what normally ends that process once arousal fades. Blocking that enzyme keeps blood in place, which makes erections easier to get and keep. Arousal still has to happen. These pills do not raise desire on their own, and both are FDA-approved for ED.

    Who ED Treatment Is For

    ED treatment is for adult men who regularly have trouble getting or keeping an erection firm enough for sex, whether it started gradually over years or showed up after a stressful stretch. Roughly half of men over 40 report some degree of ED. Younger men get it too. Anxiety, poor sleep and alcohol often play a part, and a low dose can help.

    Dosing and Schedule in Tracy

    Sildenafil is usually taken 30 to 60 minutes before sex in 25, 50 or 100 mg doses, and tadalafil as needed comes in 10 or 20 mg doses that can work for up to 36 hours. A low daily tadalafil dose of 2.5 or 5 mg skips the timing entirely. One dose per day, maximum. After 4 to 6 attempts, the dose may move up or down.

    The First Few Weeks on ED Medication

    Many men notice a difference the first time, but the honest advice from most providers is to give a new medication at least 4 to 6 tries before judging it, since nerves often get in the way early. Timing and food make a big difference with sildenafil. Track what works. If you are on daily tadalafil, expect steady effect after roughly 5 days.

    Side Effects and Safety in Tracy

    The most common side effects are headache, facial flushing, stuffy nose and upset stomach, and they usually fade within a few hours or get milder after the first couple of weeks. Some men on tadalafil notice a sore lower back the next day. Sildenafil can tint vision blue. Combining PDE5 inhibitors with nitroglycerin or amyl nitrite poppers can drop blood pressure to dangerous levels, and 4 hours means the ER.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The questionnaire covers heart health, blood pressure, chest pain with activity, liver and kidney disease, and every medication you take, including anything bought online for performance. Nitroglycerin, isosorbide and riociguat rule out sildenafil and tadalafil. Recent heart events need clearance. Within 6 months of a heart attack or stroke, your cardiologist decides.

    Sildenafil Versus Tadalafil in Tracy

    Both drugs work the same way and are similarly effective for most men, so the choice usually comes down to timing, food, side effects and how spontaneous your sex life tends to be. For sex a few times a month, sildenafil is often enough. Tadalafil gives a longer window. Compounded chewables and troches exist for men who dislike pills, but they are not FDA-approved.
