    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Pittsburg, CA

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Pittsburg

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. Lower DHT protects follicles from the slow miniaturization behind pattern loss. Minoxidil adds something different. It extends the growth phase of each hair and supports thicker strands, likely by improving blood flow and signaling at the follicle.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. Early treatment keeps the most hair, since long-bald spots seldom respond. Some women past menopause use it off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Pittsburg

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Minoxidil comes as a twice-daily topical or a low-dose off-label tablet. One spray daily also works. A compounded topical that combines finasteride and minoxidil goes on the scalp once a day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. That early shed usually comes from minoxidil pushing resting hairs out so new growing hairs can take their place, and it typically settles by month three. By months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening at the crown. In the original finasteride trials, most men kept their hair over five years, and many saw regrowth. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Pittsburg

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. These usually resolve after stopping, and rare mood changes, including low mood, should be reported to your provider right away. Scalp dryness is the usual topical issue. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention or a quicker pulse. The spray may reduce systemic side effects.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. Expect questions about your medication list and family plans, because both can shape the choice. Finasteride lowers PSA readings. Pregnant partners should not touch broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Pittsburg

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. When finasteride falls short, dutasteride is the usual next step, off-label for hair. For men avoiding DHT blockers, there is a minoxidil and GHK-Cu blend. Minoxidil alone helps, but it does nothing about DHT.
